Chief Editor

Editorial Leadership · Human Rights Advocacy · International Engagement

Sardar Nasir Aziz Khan serves as Chief Editor of The Mirror Jammu Kashmir. He brings regional insight, political clarity, and a firm commitment to principled journalism.

Originally from Rawalakot in Pakistan administered Jammu and Kashmir, he currently lives in exile in Switzerland. His editorial leadership reflects both lived experience and sustained international engagement.

In addition to his editorial responsibilities, he serves as Central Spokesperson of the United Kashmir People’s National Party and represents the party and affiliated civil society organizations at the United Nations in Geneva.

He regularly engages with United Nations mechanisms, diplomats, and international media to document and raise awareness about human rights violations, restrictions on civil liberties, and political repression.

His work focuses on issues often underreported, including enforced disappearances, arbitrary detentions, limitations on press freedom, and the erosion of democratic participation.

As Chief Editor, his responsibility is to safeguard editorial independence and ensure that journalism remains accountable to truth, not power.

Journalism must serve the public interest. It must remain independent, factual, and guided by principle.
