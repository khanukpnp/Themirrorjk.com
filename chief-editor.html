CHIEF EDITOR

Sardar Nasir Aziz Khan

Chief Editor, The Mirror Jammu Kashmir | Jammu Kashmir & Switzerland

Sardar Nasir Aziz Khan serves as Chief Editor of The Mirror Jammu Kashmir. He brings deep regional understanding, political clarity, and a strong commitment to human rights journalism. He is a Kashmiri human rights defender, political analyst, and columnist originally from Rawalakot in Pakistan administered Jammu and Kashmir. He currently lives in exile in Switzerland.

Alongside his editorial responsibilities, he is the Central Spokesperson of the United Kashmir People’s National Party and the main representative of the party and its affiliated civil society organizations to the United Nations in Geneva. In this role, he regularly engages with United Nations mechanisms, diplomats, and international media to document and highlight human rights violations and political repression.

His work focuses on issues that remain underreported or deliberately suppressed, including enforced disappearances, arbitrary detentions, restrictions on press freedom, and the erosion of civil and political rights.
