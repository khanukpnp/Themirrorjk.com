Complete Shutter Down Paralyzes Rawalakot, Poonch

Rawalakot, Poonch, Azad Kashmir 16 December 2025

A complete shutter down brought life to a standstill in Rawalakot on Monday as thousands of residents from across District Poonch poured into the streets to protest prolonged power outages, severe load shedding, low voltage electricity, and persistent internet and mobile network disruptions.

Markets remained closed and traffic was largely halted as protesters marched through the city and gathered near the grid station, demanding an immediate end to what they described as collective punishment of the population.

Residents say Azad Kashmir has been subjected to extreme load shedding for several months, with the Poonch region facing between 15 and 20 hours of power cuts daily.

The impact has been widespread. Small businesses, hospitals, schools, and universities report serious disruptions. Economic activity across the region has been pushed to the brink.

Protesters announced an indefinite sit in in Rawalakot, stressing that all agreements made with the public must be honored in full and without delay.
