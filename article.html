Complete Shutter Down Paralyzes Rawalakot as Thousands Protest Power Cuts and Blackouts

Rawalakot, Poonch, Azad Kashmir · 16 December 2025 · 7 min read

A complete shutter down brought life to a standstill in Rawalakot as thousands of residents from across District Poonch poured into the streets to protest prolonged power outages and communication blackouts.

Markets remained closed and traffic was largely halted as demonstrators gathered near the grid station, calling the situation collective punishment of the population.

Public frustration has been building for more than two years, with repeated protests over electricity shortages, low voltage supply, and failing communication networks.
